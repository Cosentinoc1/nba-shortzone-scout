What do A–F, evidence score, and position estimate mean?

VS DEF: A/B supports that row’s MORE or LESS direction; C is mixed; D/F is unfavorable. The separate overall evidence score combines history, opportunity and projected role, and is not a predicted win %. Position estimates use published fantasy eligibility and can differ from the real matchup. A high grade is not permission to skip the final injury, minutes, exact-line and payout checks.
